## Continuous voiceover — the only canon story

Today's prize medal was dated last year. I'd won our community race. My medal had a nick beside the date. I photographed it before our trusted organizer boxed it. At home, the edge was smooth. Then I found a plastic seam around the back. Mine had been solid metal. The box had stayed sealed since the organizer packed it. Maybe he'd mixed up the boxes. I returned to ask. He pointed to an old display medal beneath his desk. But its ribbon matched mine. I asked him to turn it over. There was my nick, beside today's date, exactly like my photo. My metal prize was his display. He'd boxed the plastic copy. He returned my medal. I showed the club both, and they reassigned prize handling. Right medal. Right date. Wrong organizer.
